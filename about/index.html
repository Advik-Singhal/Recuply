WHY RECUPLY

Because the little things
aren’t always little.

A favourite water bottle. A pencil case full of familiar things. A school bag that carries more than books. When something goes missing, getting it back matters.

OUR STARTING POINT

Technology with a human purpose.

Recuply began with a simple question: what if finding a lost item didn’t depend on knowing exactly where to look or exactly how to describe it?

We’re exploring how AI can help bridge the gap between a school’s photo of a found item and a family’s description of what they’ve lost.

We’re starting with schools, learning from the people who would use it, and building towards a more thoughtful lost-and-found experience.

WHAT GUIDES US

Useful. Clear. Human.

01

Start with people.

Understand real school routines before deciding what to build.

02

Keep it simple.

Make the next step easy to understand for staff and families.

03

Build thoughtfully.

Make privacy and human oversight part of the conversation from the start.
