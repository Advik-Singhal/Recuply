THE RECUPLY EXPERIENCE

From “it’s missing”
to “there it is.”

A thoughtful connection between the people who find things and the people looking for them. Here’s the experience we’re developing.

01

A school finds an item.

Staff photograph it and upload the image. AI helps generate a detailed description, which staff can review and edit.

02

Someone starts a search.

A parent or student describes the missing item in their own words, using whatever details they remember.

03

Possible matches appear.

Recuply is designed to surface likely matches. A match is a helpful lead, not a guarantee of ownership.

04

The school helps reunite them.

Clear collection instructions guide the next step. School staff remain responsible for verifying claims and handing over items.

BUILT WITH CARE

Thoughtful by design.

Every school community works a little differently. We’re designing around staff control, useful descriptions and a straightforward collection process.

Arabic-language support is part of the planned experience, with details to be tested as the product develops.
