A little more about the idea behind Recuply and what we’re working towards.

What is Recuply?

Recuply is an early-stage lost-and-found project designed for schools. The aim is to make it easier for school communities to reconnect people with their belongings.

How is it intended to work?

School staff would upload a photo of a found item and review an AI-generated description. Parents or students could then describe a missing item in their own words to see possible matches and collection instructions.

Is Recuply available now?

Recuply is in development. We’re exploring a school-first pilot; availability and launch dates have not been announced.

Who is it for?

We’re starting with schools, designing a workflow for staff and a simple search experience for parents and students.

How are items collected?

The planned experience provides clear collection instructions. School staff would verify claims and manage handover according to their own process.

Will Recuply support Arabic?

Arabic-language support is part of the planned experience. The details will be tested as the product develops.

What about privacy?

Privacy and school-controlled collection are part of the planned design. Specific policies and safeguards will be worked out before a pilot uses real data.

How much will it cost?

Pricing has not been decided. We’re still learning what schools need.
